Journey planner

Where are you headed?

Pick two stations on Lines 1, 2A or 7. We compare up to three routes - the fastest, the one with fewer reported crowds, and the one with fewer changes - and say plainly when they are the same.

What the options mean

Fastest
Lowest estimated time: about 2 minutes between stops plus estimated interchange walks and any verified delays.
Less crowded
Adds a penalty for stops with a current crowd report (moderate +2, high +6, very high +12 min). No report means no penalty - and we tell you crowding is unknown.
Fewer transfers
Counts every interchange as 20 extra minutes, so it only stays on one line when that is reasonable.
